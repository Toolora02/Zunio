What Does “Chopped” Mean?

“Chopped” is internet slang that can be used to describe someone as unattractive or looking worse than expected.

What does “chopped” mean?

Online, calling someone “chopped” is usually a negative or teasing comment about their appearance.

The word is often used casually or jokingly in social-media conversations, especially when people are comparing someone's appearance.

How is “chopped” used?

People may use “chopped” to tease someone about their appearance or to say that someone does not look as good as they expected.

Example: “Bro looked chopped in that picture 😭”

Is “chopped” an insult?

It can be. Because it usually refers negatively to someone's appearance, it may be insulting. Among friends, however, it can also be used as playful teasing.

What is the opposite of “chopped”?

Depending on the context, people may use terms such as “handsome,” “pretty,” “attractive,” or other positive slang to describe someone who looks good.

Simple meaning

Chopped = slang for looking unattractive or noticeably worse in appearance, usually said as a joke or insult.
