What Does “Brain Rot” Mean?

“Brain rot” is an internet slang term used to describe excessive exposure to online content, especially repetitive, trivial, or highly addictive content.

What does “brain rot” mean?

Online, “brain rot” can describe the feeling of spending so much time consuming internet content that it starts to feel mentally overwhelming, repetitive, or difficult to stop watching.

It can also refer to a particular piece of bizarre, repetitive, or highly viral online content.

How is “brain rot” used?

People often use the phrase jokingly after spending a lot of time watching short videos, memes, trends, or other repetitive internet content.

Example: “I've been watching these videos for three hours. My brain is actually rotting 😭”

Is “brain rot” literal?

No. In internet slang, “brain rot” is generally figurative and humorous. It does not literally mean that someone's brain is physically damaged.

Why do people say “brain rot”?

Internet users often use exaggerated language to describe how consuming large amounts of repetitive online content makes them feel.

Simple meaning

Brain rot = slang for excessive or obsessive consumption of online content, or content that feels extremely repetitive, silly, or addictive.
