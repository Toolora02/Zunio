What Does “Locked In” Mean?

“Locked in” is internet slang for being extremely focused, determined, or committed to a task or goal.

What does “locked in” mean?

When someone says they are “locked in,” they usually mean they are concentrating seriously and avoiding distractions so they can achieve something.

The phrase can describe studying, working, exercising, gaming, competing, or preparing for an important event.
