What Does “Locked In” Mean?

“Locked in” is internet slang for being extremely focused, determined, or committed to a task or goal.

What does “locked in” mean?

When someone says they are “locked in,” they usually mean they are concentrating seriously and avoiding distractions so they can achieve something.

The phrase can describe studying, working, exercising, gaming, competing, or preparing for an important event.

Examples

“I have an exam tomorrow. I'm locked in.”

“Bro has been training every day. He's locked in.”

How is “locked in” used online?

People often use “locked in” in social-media posts, comments, memes, gaming communities, and conversations about achieving a goal.

It can also be used humorously when someone suddenly becomes very serious or focused.

Is “locked in” always serious?

No. Although the phrase can describe genuine focus and determination, people frequently use it casually or humorously online.

Simple meaning

Locked in = extremely focused, determined, or committed to accomplishing something.
