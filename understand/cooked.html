What Does “Cooked” Mean?

“Cooked” is internet slang used to describe someone who is in serious trouble, has little chance of succeeding, or is in a difficult situation.

What does “cooked” mean?

When someone says “I'm cooked,” they usually mean that they are in trouble, exhausted, overwhelmed, or unlikely to recover from a situation.

The exact meaning depends on the context. It can be used seriously, but it is very often used humorously or dramatically online.

Examples

“I have an exam tomorrow and haven't studied. I'm cooked.”

“Bro missed the deadline. He's cooked 💀”

What does “I'm cooked” mean?

“I'm cooked” generally means: “I'm in trouble,” “I'm done for,” or “Things are not looking good for me.”

What does “cooking” mean?

“Cooking” can have almost the opposite meaning. Online, saying someone is “cooking” can mean they are doing something well or making good progress.

Example: “Let him cook.”

This usually means to let someone continue because they appear to be doing something successfully.
