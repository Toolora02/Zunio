Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Introduction

Zunio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use the Zunio website.

Information you provide

Zunio's basic tools do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use them.

If you contact Zunio by email, the information you include in your message may be received and used only to respond to your request or question.

Information collected automatically

Zunio does not currently require visitors to provide personal information to use its basic tools.

Website hosting or other technical services may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and deliver the website. This may include information such as browser type, device information, IP address, or pages requested.

Cookies

Zunio does not currently use cookies for advertising or analytics on its basic website tools.

If cookies or similar technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain their purpose and how they are used.

Advertising

Zunio does not currently display third-party advertisements.

If advertising is introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated with relevant information about advertising providers, cookies, similar technologies, and applicable privacy choices.

Third-party services

Zunio may use third-party services when necessary for website hosting, security, functionality, or other website operations.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Children's privacy

Zunio does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its basic website tools.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to Zunio by a child, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Zunio's features, services, or practices change. Any updates will be reflected on this page along with a revised update date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Zunio using the email address provided on the Contact page.
